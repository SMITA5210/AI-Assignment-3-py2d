Every stage sits behind its own switch in tools/versions/v15/agent.py (all off = v14 behaviour).
Each stage: unit tests + profiling + 3-match smoke + a 12-vs-12 interleaved screen against the
previous validated stage (or v14 for stage 1), screen rule predeclared in runlogs/eval/plan_v15_sN.txt.
A stage that fails its screen is switched off before the next stage starts.

Stage 1  Baseline and safety: checksums, architecture map, tests, v15 = v14 with all switches off;
         equivalence test (identical decisions to v14); screen = A/A sanity check.
Stage 2  Shared utility evaluator (STAGE_EVAL):
         - U(x, y, state): P(a shot chance from there) via the fitted v14 shot curve after a control
           delay, plus field progress; turnover risk P(lose) * danger, danger rising when fewer of our
           players are goal-side of the ball. Accepted only if it predicts "this possession scores"
           better than v14's value() on held-out replays (gates fixed before fitting).
         - Pass model refit (improvement 1): nearest-opponent pressure on the passer as a feature, player
           velocity/body in reach times; MAP with the current curve as prior; event-based labels;
           held-out gates fixed before fitting.
Stage 3  Ball-carrier lookahead (STAGE_LOOKAHEAD): hold valued with opponents closing at measured speed
         and with tackle risk; shooting now vs later on the same kick model (measured: with a >= 0.6
         shot available, v14 shot in 17 of 33 cases); search reuse between cycles (improvement 5).
Stage 4  Opponent response (STAGE_RESPONSE): one-step expectimax for the root top-6 options over the
         nearest 1-2 opponents' responses (press carrier, block shot lane, cut pass lane, close the
         receiver), probabilities counted from HELIOS replays (improvement 2, opponent model). < 5 ms.
Stage 5  CSP coordination (STAGE_CSP): hard constraints (legality: onside, in bounds, outside our box;
         distinct spots; >= 1 supporter behind the ball, 2 in our half when the ball is there);
         soft constraints (spacing, open passing lane, width); exhaustive search with pruning,
         bounded to a few thousand assignments, vectorised.
Stage 6  Off-ball lookahead (STAGE_OFFBALL): support spots scored with U (shot chance after receiving),
         through balls using receiver velocity (improvement 3); defensive anticipation of HELIOS's
         likely next pass from the opponent model (improvement 2); marking otherwise unchanged.
Later, only if the core stages are measured: MCTS on-ball (4), constant tuning by local search on
         offline objectives (6), targeted high press (7), dribble data under pressure (8).
Excluded: learning methods (instructor's warning), set pieces / goalie (framework), communication (stripped).

Final: predeclared 24 fresh interleaved matches each of v15, v14, v11 vs full; PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE as
in plan_v14_confirm.txt plus operational validity; confirmation run only after a PASS.
